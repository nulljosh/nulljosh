# Joshua Trommel

I make apps under the name [Jaybulb](https://jaybulb.com): Mac, iPhone, iPad and the web, mostly SwiftUI and Cloudflare. Most are free or one dollar. Vancouver, BC.

## A few of them

- **[Joshua Tree](https://joshuatree.jaybulb.com)**: a computer grown from seed. A kernel in C for x86-64, ARM64 and the Raspberry Pi.
- **[Turing](https://turing.jaybulb.com)**: Samantha, a small AI that lives on your Mac and does things for you. Free, private, asks first.
- **[Epiphany](https://epiphany.jaybulb.com)**: everything happening in your world on one screen. The map, the markets, the people.
- **[Nimble](https://nimble.jaybulb.com)**: instant-answer search.
- **[Echo](https://echo.jaybulb.com)**: on-device speech to text for iPhone and Mac.
- **[Plank](https://plank.jaybulb.com)**: a compiled language you can read in an evening. `brew install nulljosh/plank/plank`
- **[Vancouver Vice](https://vancouvervice.jaybulb.com)**: a crime game in the real city. Plays in your browser.
- **[Joshemon](https://joshemon.jaybulb.com)**: a monster-collecting RPG for the Game Boy Advance, written in C.

The rest are at [jaybulb.com/apps](https://jaybulb.com/apps).
